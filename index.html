Services

  • Accessibility testing and audits

    Expert accessibility testing against WCAG 2.2, with clear, prioritized findings.

  • Remediation guidance

    Practical advice for designers and developers to fix issues correctly the first time.

  • Document remediation

    Making PDFs and other documents accessible, including tagging, reading order, alt text, forms and tables.

    Professional Accessibility Conformance Report (VPAT/ACR) completion and support.

About Me

I'm Martin Berrisford, a highly experienced accessibility specialist based in the Toronto area in Canada. I have worked with a wide range of clients across North America, from small businesses to large organizations, helping them create accessible digital experiences that conform to accessibility standards.

I hold two notable professional certifications in accessibility, the IAAP Web Accessibility Specialist (WAS) and the Trusted Tester certification.

Prior to specializing in digital accessibility, I was involved in assistive technology training and support for individuals with disabilities. This experience was a valuable foundation for my work in digital accessibility.
